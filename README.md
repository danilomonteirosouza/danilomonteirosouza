## About Me

I'm an **AI Software Engineer with over 4 years of professional experience** developing backend, web, mobile, and cloud-based applications. I hold a **Master's degree in Computer Engineering** and am pursuing a **PhD in Computer Engineering at the University of Pernambuco (expected March 2027)**. My research focuses on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Neural Networks, and Explainable AI (XAI).

At **Matera**, I develop frontend and backend solutions for financial technology products, including **FlexSavings, Manager Accounting Releases, Auto Invest, and Cash Management**. I've also worked on high-performance, low-latency systems that support payments through **Pix**, Brazil's instant payment system, on platforms serving millions of customers.

My work spans **Flutter Web and Dart**, backend services and **RESTful APIs with Java and Spring Boot**, **Apache Kafka** for event-driven messaging, and **MySQL**. I deploy applications with **Docker and Kubernetes**, work with **AWS**, and use **Jenkins** for CI/CD. I also integrate authentication and authorization with **Keycloak**, **OAuth 2.0**, **OpenID Connect (OIDC)**, **Single Sign-On (SSO)**, and **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**.

I use **Clean Architecture, SOLID principles, and Test-Driven Development (TDD)**. My current development workflow combines **Scrum** with **Specification-Driven Development (SDD)** through the **BMAD Method**, supported by **Claude Code** and **OpenCode** for AI-assisted software engineering.
